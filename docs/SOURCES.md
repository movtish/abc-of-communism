# Sources

The publications below supplied the census inputs and contextual evidence. Entries record which material is included and which remains unavailable. Historical publications retain their Russian titles.

## Analysis inputs

- [Education in the 1959 census](https://www.demoscope.ru/weekly/ssp/rus_edu_59.php) and the [original workbook](https://www.demoscope.ru/weekly/ssp/rus_edu_59.xls). RGAE, fond 1562, opis 336, files 1591-1594. The workbook supplies the regional follow-up. Four downloaded regional HTML tables provide extraction checks.
- [Age in the 1939 census](https://www.demoscope.ru/weekly/ssp/rus_age_39.php) and [illiteracy in 1939](https://www.demoscope.ru/weekly/ssp/rus_ill_39.php). The scope checks use Moscow and Leningrad; further Tatar, Chuvash and Yakut tables verify inputs to the new follow-up. Saved HTML and exact comparisons are included.
- [Public Education in the USSR, volume XXVIII, issue 1](https://istmat.org/node/22102), published in 1926. The [downloaded section](https://istmat.org/files/uploads/22102/obrazovanie_1921-25_vyp.1_1924_god.pdf) contains the survey of 1 January 1924. Table XVII, printed pages 462-467, supplies the checked institutional transcription.
- [National economic plan for 1931](https://istmat.org/files/uploads/52124/plan_sssr_na_1931_god.pdf). Printed pages 67-68 give the differing rural and urban age priorities and discuss non-completion. The figures describe planned participation and reported non-completion.
- Contemporary migration tables and [political education statistics in 1932](https://www.istmat.org/node/27236), together with the [1923 time-use material](https://istmat.org/node/27934), support the contextual extracts. Their units and small-sample limitations are retained in the CSV files.

## Contextual publications

- [Preliminary results of the 1920 census, issue 1](https://istmat.org/files/uploads/26366/perepis_1920_vyp._1.pdf). Fourteen pages on population in 25 European Russian provinces. It is not a complete table of adult literacy by age.
- [Ermolaev, Sharaf and Khasanov, Essays on the Economic Geography of the Tatar Republic, 1931](https://kitaphane.tatarstan.ru/file/kitaphane/File/20.%20Inv_7416_70.pdf). The 321-page scan contains literacy comparisons, but OCR errors and changing denominators prevent adding those figures to the regressions without further visual transcription.
- [Education statistics in the 1931 handbook](https://istmat.org/files/uploads/20626/spravochnik_1931_obrazovanie.pdf). Five pages, chiefly aggregate administrative coverage. Enrolment is not completion and neither equals cohort literacy.
- [Political education in 1930-1934](https://istmat.org/files/uploads/22124/kult1930_34_polit.pdf) and [adult schools in 1935](https://istmat.org/files/uploads/22123/kultstroy_1935_shkoly_vzroslyh.pdf). Complete downloaded sections, but no harmonised panel of the main sample's regional exposure was recovered.
- [National Archive of Khakassia guide, 2016](https://arhiv.r-19.ru/upload/iblock/c3e/c3ee70255a3a696bd93c7064fc7d5177.pdf). Describes village-level population and literacy records. The guide was downloaded; the underlying archival schedules were not.

## Sources with incomplete access

- [Pskov census of 1920, sex, age and literacy](https://elib.shpl.ru/ru/nodes/59534-vyp-1-pol-vozrast-i-gramotnost-1921), with a [Presidential Library description](https://www.prlib.ru/item/330421). A viewer exposes 116 images. Selected pages were inspected, but full high-resolution retrieval returned HTTP 429. Adult age groups on inspected pages are broader than the paper's five-year windows.
- [Ivanovo-Voznesensk census of 1920](https://search.rsl.ru/ru/record/01008860487). The catalogue describes a 79-page volume on sex, age and literacy. A working full-scan link was not obtained.
- [Vologda provincial handbook, 1923](https://www.booksite.ru/fulltext/delsprav/text.pdf). Indexed text identifies literacy tables, but direct download returned HTTP 403. No complete local PDF is included and the OCR has not been visually verified.
- [General results of the 1937 census, 2007 edition](https://search.rsl.ru/ru/record/01003030415). The contents and open text locate sex-by-age literacy tables. Only the contents PDF was downloaded, not a verified full original scan. The historical processing territories must not be equated with particular cities without checking.
- [Gosplan, Universal Education, Illiteracy Eradication and Training, 1930](https://search.rsl.ru/ru/record/01009240152). The catalogue record identifies the publication. Its regional tables remain unverified.

## Archival leads

The [All-Russian Extraordinary Commission for the Eradication of Illiteracy guide](https://guides.rusarchives.ru/funds/vserossiyskaya-chrezvychaynaya-komissiya-po-likvidacii-negramotnosti-i-malogramotnosti-vchklb) describes provincial returns, surveys, budgets and credits. The commission's fond A-2314 must be distinguished from Glavpolitprosvet's A-2313. File numbers, annual coverage and surviving individual schedules require archival checking.

The [Yaroslavl archive publication](https://www.yararchive.ru/publications/details/268/) gives instructions and local references concerning registration, plans and instruction. It is evidence of administration, including compulsion before the mass campaign. It does not establish that a complete digitised individual panel survives.

Download URLs, byte counts and SHA-256 hashes for local copies are in the two supplementary source manifests. Some HTML was decoded to UTF-8 for use by the parsers; published numeric tables were not rewritten. No restricted third-party replication microdata are redistributed.
